The proper-noun wall

why 1.2 is unreachable honestly

~25% of English word-instances contain a subword that appears exactly once in the article — mostly proper nouns. BPE can’t merge a pair it sees once, so these split into many tokens. Only min_pair_freq=1 forces them into one token — i.e. memorising them.
