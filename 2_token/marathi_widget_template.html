India on Wikipedia — one BPE tokenizer for four languages

A single joint byte-pair-encoding vocabulary (budget 10,000 tokens) trained on the Wikipedia “India” article in English, Hindi, Telugu and Marathi, and evaluated on the same four articles. Fertility X = word tokens ÷ words; score = 1000 / (Xmax − Xmin).

Fertility ratio by language

X = average tokens per word on the India article (lower is better). All four languages are encoded by the same shared vocabulary. Hover a bar for details.
