Why this beats an [UNK] tokenizer

A tokenizer that maps unseen characters to [UNK] (like the posted reference) silently deletes them — its decode(encode(x)) loses accents, CJK, emoji, etc. This tokenizer reserves 256 byte-fallback tokens, so every byte of every input is representable and nothing is ever dropped. Normalization is off (NFKC/casefold also change visible characters), so bytes are preserved exactly.
